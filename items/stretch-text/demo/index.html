Case study · Grocery app, 2025

Delivery slots, moved up front

Problem

Checkout lost a third of shoppers on mobile in spring 2025, most of them at the delivery slot step, where slots loaded last and often disappeared after being picked.

What we changed

We moved the slot choice from the last step to the top of the cart and held the picked slot for 20 minutes, so prices and fees were final before payment.

Result

Drop-off at checkout fell from 34% to 19% in six weeks, and support tickets about vanished slots dropped by half.
