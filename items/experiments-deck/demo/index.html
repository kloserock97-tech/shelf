WebGL experiments

Side project 01

Windcrest

  • A hill where every blade of grass is real geometry: 380,000 of them in one draw call (instancing).
  • Wind comes in gusts, grass grows in tufts, and the cursor leaves a trail like a hand on a carpet.
  • A blade is never thinner than a pixel, so far grass does not shimmer.

Side project 02

Driftfield

  • 80,000 points drift like smoke in a draught, a curl-noise field worked out on the GPU every frame.
  • No stored frames, so time can stop and rewind like a video.
  • Background blur with a real f-stop (the thin-lens formula).

Side project 03

Nightsail

  • A giant marble head under a column of light; the tide drowns it and gives it back.
  • The stone knows the height of the same waves the sea draws, so the water touches it exactly.
  • Built on WebGPU, with shaders in TSL.

Open the live demo ↗Source ↗

Side project 04

Meadow Walk

  • An endless flight over a windy meadow: half a million blades placed and bent by the GPU.
  • The cursor sends soft waves through the field.
  • Hills, grass and shadows share one height map, updated every frame.
