A year on two wheelsScroll the story: the chart follows each step.

From January to July, monthly rides tripled: 18 thousand at the start of the year, 58 thousand at its peak.

Summer carried it. June, July and August brought 38% of all 444 thousand rides.

But winter grew fastest: December to February rides were up 39% on last year.

Per active member, the picture flips. Winter riders ride most often, 3.5 times in December against 2.3 in July.
