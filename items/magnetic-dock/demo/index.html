Scroll the page, the dock follows.

Move the cursor along the dock: items grow toward it, and the edge lights up where your hand is. Tab works too.

About

Proximity, not hover. Each item measures how far the cursor is along the dock and grows by a smoothstep of that distance, up to 16%, from its top edge down.

One animation frame drives every item. It writes a value only when it changed and goes to sleep when nothing moves.

Work

The active item is the last section whose top has passed 40% of the window. It turns to paper, like the cards.
